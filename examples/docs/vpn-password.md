# Resetting your VPN password

VPN passwords expire every 90 days. To reset yours, open the self-service portal, choose "VPN" and
then "Reset password". The new password works within 5 minutes.

If the portal says your account is locked, wait 15 minutes and try again, or call the service desk
on extension 4400. Never share your VPN password; the service desk will never ask for it.
